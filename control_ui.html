How a move works
  1. The arm must be holding (not limp), at a pose it knows (a saved view, the transit pose, or the path between them), with live data and no leader arm linked. The status strip says which of these is missing and what to do.
  2. Every move has up to three steps: rise to the transit pose (the safe raised pose), turn there, then descend into the view. At Fast and Turbo the steps may overlap high above the desk ("blended").
  3. A view that nobody has marked as checked always runs at first-run speed, whatever speed is selected. Watch it go there and back cleanly, then mark it checked.
  4. STOP (the red button, Space or Esc) holds the arm where it is. It then reports whether the arm confirmed the hold.
  5. If the arm ends up somewhere it does not know: Recover to transit lifts it straight up slowly, or go limp, pose it by hand at the transit pose and press Hold here. One person stays at the arm, power plug within reach.
