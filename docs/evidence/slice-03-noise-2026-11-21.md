# Wake and command noise suppression

## Acceptance procedure

- Replay the same clean, fan-like noise and competing-speaker fixtures through the raw and enhanced voice paths. Count correct wakes, misses and false wakes independently. Include the configured names Alexa and Google as mutual negative examples.
- Verify streaming frame output and reset across mute, session restart and repeated wakes. The command recognizer and Silero consume the same enhanced samples as the wake detector, including the rolling handoff buffer.
- Owner test on Pixel 10 Pro XL with video and recording running. From a fixed position facing the rear camera, try ten wake-and-command attempts in quiet and ten with a fan or similar noise. Record wake hits, missed wakes, correct commands and any false wakes on an unrelated name. Confirm quiet runs, mute and stream restart still work. Repeat over LAN with WAN disabled and verify the saved recording has audible audio.

Real microphone acceptance is pending.

## Host model comparison

A Linux replay used sherpa-onnx 2.0.2 and the app's Zipformer configuration at
16 kHz in 20 ms chunks. The speech was generated by Piper's US Lessac and GB
Alan voices. The 26 positive clips cover eight names with stand-alone wake
phrases or immediate “start recording” commands. The 14 negative clips use
an unrelated wake name. Fan-like noise was synthesized from filtered white
noise and low tones. Competing speech used a separate generated speaker.
Noise levels are nominal speech-to-noise RMS ratios of the input mixtures.

| Input | Raw | GTCRN | DPDFNet Baseline |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Quiet | 26/26 | 26/26 | 25/26 |
| Fan-like noise, +5 dB | 10/26 | 10/26 | 19/26 |
| Fan-like noise, 0 dB | 1/26 | 2/26 | 5/26 |
| Fan-like noise, −5 dB | 0/26 | 0/26 | 1/26 |
| Competing speaker, 0 dB | 7/26 | 9/26 | 6/26 |

Each mode produced zero false wakes on its 14 unrelated-name clips per
condition. These clips do not provide a false-wake rate for a real room.
Denoising was about 0.07–0.09× real time for DPDFNet on this host. DPDFNet was
selected for the measured stationary-noise gains. It missed one quiet clip
and one more clip under competing speech than raw input in this test.

A separate host replay exercised wake-to-ASR handoff with the production
320 ms pre-roll and the existing English ASR model. Of 12 immediate
“start recording” clips, the expected command appeared in 11 raw and 10
enhanced quiet transcripts. With a +5 dB fan mixture, it appeared in 0 raw
and 4 enhanced transcripts. This test ended the streams at fixture EOF and
did not measure live VAD timing or invoke recording.
